# Instagram Algorithm — Rules I'm Following for Truly Susi
**Locked in May 22, 2026. Re-read before drafting any post.**

These are the operating principles I apply to every recommendation for @trulysusi. If something contradicts these, flag it.

---

## 1. Posting cadence — NOT daily on the grid
- **4 grid posts per week is the sweet spot.** Mon · Wed · Fri · Sat.
- Posting daily on the grid actively **hurts** distribution — your own posts compete with each other for the same followers' feeds, and the algorithm "rests" accounts that flood the feed.
- **Consistency > frequency.** 4 every week beats 7 one week and 2 the next.
- **One grid post per day, max.** Only exception: launch day, when a second post is event-driven and expected.

## 2. Format mix per week
- **At least 1 Reel per week** — non-negotiable for a new account. Reels are the only format that reliably reaches non-followers.
- **At least 1 carousel per week** — carousels have the highest dwell time, which the algorithm weighs heavily.
- The rest can be single-image posts (product hero, culture).

## 3. Stories are different — daily, multiple
- Stories don't compete with each other the way grid posts do.
- **Post 1–5 Stories per day.** Daily Story = bare minimum.
- Stories build retention with existing followers; they don't bring new ones.
- **Countdown stickers, polls, question boxes, quizzes** — every Story should ideally have a sticker. Stickers double engagement signal to the algo.

## 4. Timing
- **Post 7–9 PM IST** (already locked in your schedule). This is the sweet spot for Indian audiences.
- For launch day, **11 AM** is the right call — gives the post all day to spread before the evening peak.
- Be online on the account **30 min before → 90 min after** every post. The algorithm runs a "test distribution" in the first hour — if engagement is high, it pushes wider.

## 5. What the algorithm actually rewards (in priority order)
1. **Sends & shares to DMs** — the strongest signal in 2025/26. Write captions that beg to be sent to someone.
2. **Saves** — for recipes, how-tos, educational carousels.
3. **Comments** — captions ending in a question outperform statements.
4. **Watch time** (Reels) — completion rate beats length. 7–15 second Reels often beat 30s+.
5. **Likes** — least valuable signal. Don't optimise for these.

## 6. Captions
- **First 8 words = the hook.** Instagram cuts the rest with "...more". Earn the tap.
- **End with a question** when possible — comments are a top algo signal.
- One idea per caption. If there are two ideas, that's two posts.
- **No more than 8–12 hashtags.** Mix small (under 50k posts), medium, and one or two big ones. Rotate — don't repeat the same set on consecutive posts.

## 7. Carousels
- Aim for **7–10 slides**. The algorithm re-serves your carousel as a different "first slide" if a user scrolled past — more chances to catch them.
- Slide 1 = hook. Slide 2–N = build. Last slide = CTA.
- Save-worthy content (lists, recipes, how-tos) lives on carousels.

## 8. Reels
- **7–25 seconds**, ideally under 15. Completion rate is the #1 signal.
- **First 2 seconds must hook** — no slow build-ups.
- Add subtitles always (most people watch with sound off).
- Use a trending audio if it fits. Branded original audio is fine once you have scale.

## 9. Things to NOT do
- **Don't delete posts.** Even underperforming ones. Archive instead — deletion signals instability and hurts overall account reach.
- **Don't post a Reel as a regular feed video.** Always upload as Reel for distribution.
- **Don't tag irrelevant accounts** to try and piggyback reach. Tags from real collabs are gold; spam tags get the post throttled.
- **Don't post and leave.** Engage with the first wave of comments in the first hour.
- **Don't go silent for 7+ days.** The algorithm "cools" your account. If you have to pause, post a Story even if you can't post a grid.

## 10. Growth tactics for the first 30 days
- **Collab posts** — IG's collab feature shares one post across two grids and pools both audiences. Find one complementary creator (Tamil food blogger, Salem/Coimbatore lifestyle account) and do a collab in week 1 post-launch.
- **Reply to every DM and comment in week 1.** This trains the algorithm that the account is "alive."
- **Story shoutouts** — anyone who tags you, reshare within 30 minutes. It's free social proof and trains the algo that your content gets shared.
- **Local hashtags > generic ones.** #SalemSweets, #SalemFoodie, #SalemTamilNadu will out-deliver #SweetsOfIndia for you, every time. (If you ship to Chennai/Bangalore, add those city tags only on posts where shipping is being talked about.)

## 11. Posting schedule for Truly Susi (locked)
- **Monday** — product photo + Story
- **Wednesday** — Reel + Story
- **Friday** — culture post + Story
- **Saturday** — carousel + Story
- **Daily** — minimum 1 Story
- **Time** — 7–9 PM IST (11 AM on launch day)

This is the rule. Anything I recommend has to fit this cadence unless we're treating a day as an event (launch day, festival day, etc.).
