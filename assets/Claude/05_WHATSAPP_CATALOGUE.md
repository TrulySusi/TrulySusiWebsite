# WhatsApp Business Catalogue — Setup Guide
**To complete by: Wednesday May 27 evening (24 hours before launch).**

The WhatsApp catalogue is the silent salesperson during launch — every DM that comes in will be answered by it before you type a word. Get this right and you'll save hours of repetitive replies on launch day.

---

## Step 1 · WhatsApp Business setup (if not already done)
1. Download **WhatsApp Business** (separate app from regular WhatsApp).
2. Use the number you want customers to order on — once chosen, don't change it.
3. Set the business profile:
   - **Business name:** Truly Susi
   - **Category:** Food & Beverage
   - **Description:** *"Homemade Tamil sweets, made by Susi in Salem. Mysore Pak · Badam Halwa · Thenkulal. Sweeter together. 🤍"*
   - **Address:** Salem, Tamil Nadu (full address optional — depends on whether you want pickup)
   - **Hours:** Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM
   - **Email:** (your contact email)
   - **Website:** https://instagram.com/trulysusi (until you have a site)
   - **Profile photo:** The kuruvi logo, lavender background

## Step 3 · Automated messages

### Greeting message (auto-sends on first message)
> Hi! 🤍 Welcome to Truly Susi.
>
> We make homemade Tamil sweets in Salem — three recipes from Susi's family kitchen.
>
> Tap "View catalogue" below to see what's available.
> When you're ready to order, just send us the items + your delivery area and we'll confirm within an hour.

### Away message (outside business hours)
> Hi 🤍 We're away from the kitchen right now but we'll reply first thing in the morning (we open at 9 AM).
>
> Meanwhile, tap "View catalogue" below to see what we're making this week.

### Quick replies (set up these shortcuts)
- **/order** — *"Got it! To confirm: [items + quantity], delivery to [area], on [date]. Total: ₹___. Payment via [UPI/Cash on delivery]. Sound good?"*
- **/delivery** — *"We deliver across Salem. Same-day delivery (orders before 12 PM) — ₹___. Next-day delivery — ₹___. Shipping outside Salem? DM us and we'll work it out."*
- **/festival** — *"Festival boxes are made to order — we usually need 3–4 days notice. DM us with the festival date and number of boxes and we'll put together a quote."*
- **/thanks** — *"Thank you so much 🤍 Susi will be smiling when we tell her. Tag us on @trulysusi if you share — we love seeing where the sweets end up."*

## Step 4 · Sharable catalogue link

Once the catalogue is live, WhatsApp gives you a `wa.me/c/<your-number>` link. **Put this in your Instagram bio** as the primary CTA:

> A story born from a mother's love 🤍
> Order on WhatsApp ↓

---

## Step 5 · Things to test before launch day

- [ ] Send yourself a test order. Does the greeting fire? Is the catalogue easy to browse?
- [ ] Have a friend on a different phone test the flow. Time it: from "first message" to "order confirmed" should be under 3 minutes.
- [ ] Make sure the catalogue link works from an Instagram bio tap (some old phones don't render `wa.me/c/` links well — have a backup direct number ready).
- [ ] Check that all four products appear in the right order.
- [ ] Pricing is correct on every variant.
- [ ] Auto-reply messages send instantly.
