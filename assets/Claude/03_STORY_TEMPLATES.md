# Story Templates — Launch Week
**Daily Story copy for May 22 → May 31 (and a repeating pattern for post-launch).**

Stories carry more weight than the grid this week. The grid is the magazine; Stories are the radio — on all day, intimate, always live.

Rules:
- **Minimum 1 Story per day**, ideally 3–5 on launch day and the days flanking it.
- **Always pair a grid post with at least one Story** ("tap to see new post" with the sticker).
- **Use stickers liberally** — polls, questions, quizzes, "tap to react." Each sticker doubles engagement.
- **Story duration ~5–7 seconds** per frame. Tap-pause if you have more to say.
- **Save the best Stories to Highlights** — Our Story / Sweets / How to Order / Festivals.

## Repeatable Story patterns (post-launch)

After the launch week's emotional peak, these formats sustain engagement without burning Susi out:

1. **#KitchenWindow** — once a week, a single Story showing what's on the stove or the counter. No words. No production. Just a moment.
2. **#AmmaSaid** — a quote sticker once a week with something Susi has actually said about food, ingredients, or sharing. Build a Highlight for these.
3. **#OneBoxOneStory** — every restock, share one customer story (with permission). Real names, real reasons, real moments.
4. **#FilterCoffeeFridays** — every Friday, a photo of the sweet of the week with a filter coffee on the side. A small, repeatable ritual the audience starts to expect.
5. **#WhatYourAmmaMakes** — monthly question sticker. The answers become content for the next month.

## What goes into each Highlight

Save Stories to Highlights as you go — don't wait until the end of launch week.

**Our Story** — Meet Susi carousel reshare, Susi's hands clip, a "why we started" quote frame, the family kitchen shot.

**Sweets** — The three product hero photos, close-ups, how-to-eat tips, ingredient stories.

**How to Order** — Step-by-step Story sequence: "1. DM us or WhatsApp. 2. We confirm within 1 hour. 3. We make. 4. We deliver."

**Festivals** — Empty until your first festival post. Build this Highlight over time around Vinayagar Chaturthi, Karthikai, Diwali, Pongal, Tamil New Year.
