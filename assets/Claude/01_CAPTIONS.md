## Caption rules cheat sheet

- Hook = first 8 words. Earn the tap.
- One idea per caption. If you have two, write two posts.
- No emojis in the hook. One 🤍 at the end is the brand signature.
- Never say "premium," "authentic," or "best." Let the photo say it.
- CTAs go on launch + product hero posts only. Culture posts stay culture posts.
- Always end product captions with **"Order on WhatsApp (link in bio)"** — exact phrasing, every time.
