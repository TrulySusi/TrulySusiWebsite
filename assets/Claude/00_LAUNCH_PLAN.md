## How this plan works

Two phases:

**Phase 1 — Pre-launch countdown (May 22 → May 27)** · build anticipation, introduce Susi as a real person, give people a reason to care before the sweets arrive.

**Phase 2 — Launch + first 10 days (May 28 → June 7)** · convert curiosity into orders. Heavy product storytelling, social proof as soon as the first orders go out.

Every post follows the schedule already locked in: **post between 7–9 PM IST**, at least one Story every day, and a Story to support every grid post (sticker → "tap to see new post").

## Tone reminders (read this before writing any caption)

- Susi is a **mother**, not a brand persona. Write the way you'd describe your own mother to a friend who has never met her — proud, specific, a little tender, never overdone.
- The brand voice is **warm but never sentimental**. Avoid "love" as a filler word. Use it only when you mean it.
- **Short captions outperform long ones at launch.** Save the long-form storytelling for the carousel slides.
- Don't pair Tamil words with English explanations in parentheses. Trust the audience or use a footnote slide.
- Never call yourself "premium." Let the photography do that work.
