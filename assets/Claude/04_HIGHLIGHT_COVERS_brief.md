# Story Highlight Covers — Design Brief
**4 covers · 1080 × 1920 (cropped to circle in-app)**
**For the designer who built the rest of the brand system.**

---

## Why this matters

Highlight covers are the first thing a new follower sees under your bio. Right now they're empty circles. With the brand's warmth, the covers should feel like four small windows into the world — each one quiet, branded, and inviting.

## Design system

- **Shape:** Use the **arched window** from the kuruvi logo as the framing motif on every cover. Each cover = a different scene inside the arch.
- **Background:** Alternate between **Lavender #EACAE8** and **Blush #FDECE2** so the four covers form a visible pair-pattern in the profile.
- **Inner illustration:** A single, simple icon or silhouette in **Navy #041C35** or **Red #E2372B**. Flat, line-led, no gradients.
- **No text** on the cover itself — Instagram puts the Highlight title underneath. Keep the visual clean.
- **Safe area:** Keep all visual content within the centre 60% of the canvas. Instagram crops aggressively to circle.

---

## Cover 1 · Our Story
- **Background:** Lavender #EACAE8
- **Inside the arch:** A small silhouette of Susi's hand (a wrist with bangles, holding a wooden spoon, or stirring). Navy outline.
- **Mood:** Personal. Quiet.
- **Highlight title:** `Our Story`

## Cover 2 · Sweets
- **Background:** Blush #FDECE2
- **Inside the arch:** A small flat-lay illustration of three round sweets (representing Mysore Pak, Badam Halwa, Thenkulal). Red #E2372B fills, navy outlines.
- **Mood:** Inviting. A little playful.
- **Highlight title:** `Sweets`

## Cover 3 · How to Order
- **Background:** Lavender #EACAE8
- **Inside the arch:** A simple line illustration of a WhatsApp chat bubble or a paper-wrapped sweet with a "ribbon" detail. Navy.
- **Mood:** Clear. Functional but still warm.
- **Highlight title:** `Order`

## Cover 4 · Festivals
- **Background:** Blush #FDECE2
- **Inside the arch:** A simple oil lamp / diya silhouette, or a kolam pattern circle. Red.
- **Mood:** Festive. Anchored in tradition.
- **Highlight title:** `Festivals`

---

## Order in profile (left to right)
1. Our Story
2. Sweets
3. Order
4. Festivals

Reason: a new visitor reads them like a sentence — *who you are → what you make → how to get it → when to think of you again.*
