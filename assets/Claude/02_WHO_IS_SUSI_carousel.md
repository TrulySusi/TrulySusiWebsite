# "Meet Susi" Carousel — Copy
**Posting slot: Monday May 25 · 8:30 PM IST (3 days before launch)**
**Format: 7-slide carousel · 1080 × 1350 (4:5)**

This is the most important post of launch week. It earns the follow before the sweets do the rest. Designed to feel intimate — not a product post.

---

## Visual direction for the designer

- Slide background: alternate between **Lavender #EACAE8** and **Blush #FDECE2** — soft, never the loud red.
- All headlines in **Operetta 18** · navy (#041C35).
- Body copy in **Acumin Variable** · navy, generous line height.
- Use the **arch motif** from the kuruvi logo as a quiet framing device on slides 2 and 6.
- If you have a portrait of Susi (even casual / candid), it goes on slide 4. If not, use her hands — a wrist with bangles, a hand stirring ghee, a hand folding butter paper. Hands are almost more powerful here.
- No product shots in this carousel. This is about Susi, not the sweets.

**Body:**

### Slide 3 · Introduce her
**Headline:**
She isn't a chef.
She's a mother.

**Body:**
Susi has spent four decades quietly feeding the people she loves — children, grandchildren, neighbours who turned up at the door, friends who needed something sweet on a hard day.

Nobody asked for a brand.
We just thought it was time more people got to taste it.

---

### Slide 4 · The portrait *(image-led, less copy)*
**Image:** Susi's hands at work — folding, stirring, or shaping. Soft natural light.

**Overlay text (small, bottom-right):**
"If you're going to make sweets, make them like you'd give them to your own."
— Susi

### Slide 5 · The three sweets
**Headline:**
She's starting with the three she's been perfecting the longest.

Badam Halwa — slow-cooked almonds, deep colour, made the day you eat it.
Thenkulal — the festival sweet most people have forgotten. She hasn't.

## Why this post matters more than any other

People don't follow food brands because of food. They follow them because of people. This carousel is the only chance you have during launch week to turn Susi from an idea into a person — and to turn @trulysusi from a profile into a story they want to be part of.

Don't skip slide 4. The portrait (or the hands) is the slide that earns the share.
