Our Story

Meet Susi.

She is someone who brings warmth into a room before she's even said hello. Someone who feels the people around her — notices the quiet ones, asks about your day before her own, and remembers the one thing you mentioned liking six months ago.

Feeding people is what makes her happiest. Not because anyone asked her to. Because she gets to.

Her cooking has been known in this family, on this street, for decades. Not because she was selling it. Just because she made it. And people kept coming back for more.

"Some sweets you eat. This one, you remember."
