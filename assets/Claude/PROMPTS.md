## Prompt #1 · Trending Audio Scan

**Cadence:** Every **Tuesday** and **Thursday**
**Task:**
Scan Instagram and identify any trending audio, songs, or sounds that have recently moved from **under 1,000 uses to over 50,000 uses** (the "rising fast" window — most valuable for catching a trend before it peaks).

**Output format:**
A clean list, with each entry containing:
- Track / sound name
- Original artist or movie reference (if any)
- Current Reel-use count
- Approximate growth window (e.g. *"under 1k → 60k in 4 days"*)
- Why it might fit Truly Susi specifically (mother / heritage / food / Tamil-coded / soft instrumental)
- A 1-line suggestion of which Reel format on Truly Susi this audio would suit

**Why this matters:** Catching audio in the 1k → 50k window means we use it while the algorithm is still actively pushing posts that use it. Late-trend audio (over 500k) is already in the "exit phase" and gives far less reach. This is one of the most under-used moves for new accounts.
